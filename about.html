About This Website

📘 About

Student Academic Calculator is an unofficial website developed to help students calculate their GPA, CGPA, target CGPA, and find course credits quickly and accurately.

⭐ Features

  • Semester GPA Calculator
  • Overall CGPA Calculator
  • Target CGPA Calculator
  • Find Course Credits
  • Easy-to-use interface

⚠ Disclaimer

This website is an independent student project created for educational purposes. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially maintained by any university or educational institution. Please verify official academic information with your university.

🎯 Purpose

The goal of this website is to provide students with a simple and reliable academic calculator that saves time and makes GPA and CGPA calculations easier.
