Help

📊 GPA Calculator

The GPA Calculator helps you calculate your semester GPA.

  1. Enter the Course Code or manually enter the Course Credit.
  2. Select the grade obtained for each subject.
  3. Click Calculate GPA.
  4. Your semester GPA will be displayed instantly.

🎓 CGPA Calculator

Use this calculator to determine your overall CGPA.

  1. Enter the GPA of each completed semester.
  2. Enter the total credits for each semester.
  3. Click Calculate CGPA.
  4. Your overall CGPA will be displayed.

🎯 Target CGPA Calculator

This calculator estimates the GPA you need in your next semester to achieve your target CGPA.

  1. Enter your Current CGPA.
  2. Enter the Credits Completed.
  3. Enter your Target CGPA.
  4. Enter the Credits of the Upcoming Semester.
  5. Click Calculate.

📚 Find Course Credits

Find the credit value of a course using its course code.

  1. Enter the Course Code (Example: CHY1008).
  2. Click Find Credit.
  3. The corresponding course credit will be displayed.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Is this an official university website?

No. This is an unofficial student project created for educational purposes.

Q. Are the calculations accurate?

The calculations follow the formulas used in the website. Always verify your results with your university's official academic records.

Q. What should I do if a course code is missing?

If a course code is not available, you can manually enter the course credit when calculating your GPA.

📌 Tips

  • Always double-check the grades you enter.
  • Verify that course credits match your university curriculum.
  • Use the Target CGPA Calculator to plan future semesters.
  • Keep your course list updated for accurate calculations.
